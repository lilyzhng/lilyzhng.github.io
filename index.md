  <div class="intro-text">
    <p>
      I’m Lily Zhang (publishing as Xianling Zhang), a tech lead and research scientist in the Bay Area, California.
      I’ve published at NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, and RAL
      <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15447">[1]</a>
      <a href="https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Zhang_SIMBAR_Single_Image-Based_Scene_Relighting_for_Effective_Data_Augmentation_for_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf">[2]</a>
      <a href="https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023W/OODCV/papers/Shrivastava_DatasetEquity_Are_All_Samples_Created_Equal_In_The_Quest_For_ICCVW_2023_paper.pdf">[3]</a>
      <a href="https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022W/VDU/papers/Jaipuria_deepPIC_Deep_Perceptual_Image_Clustering_for_Identifying_Bias_in_Vision_CVPRW_2022_paper.pdf">[4]</a>
      <a href="https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPRW_2020/papers/w45/Jaipuria_Deflating_Dataset_Bias_Using_Synthetic_Data_Augmentation_CVPRW_2020_paper.pdf">[5]</a>,
      and gave a keynote at NVIDIA GTC on post-training with synthetic data.
    </p>
    <p>
      At <a href="https://lat.ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Latitude AI</a>, I build LLMs, multimodal LLMs, and AI agents for physical AI.
      Previously, I led applied R&D at Ford Greenfield Labs (2019–2023) <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-lab-road-turning-self-driving-research-production-zhang/?trackingId=eV%2FKTo9HTx6STl2nFehbLA%3D%3D">on training perception models and synthetic data</a>, and was a Machine Learning Engineer at BMW working on perception.
    </p>
    <p>
      My latest research reframes <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25297" target="_blank" rel="noopener">feature engineering as agentic code generation</a> (Eureka), in collaboration with Alibaba Cloud: an SFT + RL post-trained AI-infra agent deployed at Alibaba Cloud with 91% adoption.
      <a href="https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/loc/mexico-city/129981">Poster at NeurIPS 2025 VLM4RWD</a>; <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-92-0378-9_33" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Oral at DASFAA 2026</a>.
    </p>
    <p>
      You can reach me at lilyzhng.ai AT gmail.com.
    </p>
